ForexAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

ForexAI provides Forex market analysis, market signals, and risk calculation tools. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

2. Information We Process

  • API key: You provide a Twelve Data API key to retrieve market data.
  • Market preferences: Currency pairs and timeframes selected by you.
  • Signal history: Previous scanner results saved locally on your device.

ForexAI does not currently require an account, name, or password.

3. Third-Party Services

ForexAI uses Twelve Data to retrieve Forex market information. Requests may include your API key, selected currency pair, and timeframe. Twelve Data handles information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

Signal history is stored locally on your device. ForexAI does not currently operate its own user account system or cloud database.

ForexAI does not sell personal information.

5. Security

Please protect your API key and do not share it publicly. The current prototype sends requests directly from your device, so the API key may be visible in browser or network activity.

6. Financial Disclaimer

ForexAI provides informational market analysis only. It does not provide guaranteed trading results or personalized financial advice. Trading Forex involves significant risk.

7. Children

ForexAI is not designed specifically for children.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as ForexAI develops. The latest version will be published on this page.
