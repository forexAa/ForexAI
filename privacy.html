ForexAI Privacy Policy Effective date: November 9, 2026 1. Introduction ForexAI (“the App”) provides Forex market analysis, market signals, and risk-calculation tools. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App. 2. Information and data ForexAI may process the following information: - Twelve Data API key: You enter your API key to request market data. The App uses it to communicate with Twelve Data. - Market preferences: Selected currency pairs and timeframes are used to retrieve market information. - Signal history: Previous scan results may be saved locally on your device using browser storage. ForexAI does not currently require an account, name, or password. 3. Third-party services ForexAI uses Twelve Data to retrieve Forex market data. Requests may include your API key, selected currency pair, and timeframe. Twelve Data processes information according to its own privacy policy and terms. 4. Data storage and sharing Signal history is stored locally on your device. ForexAI does not currently operate its own user account or cloud database. ForexAI does not sell personal information. 5. Security Please protect your API key. Do not share it publicly. The current app prototype sends API requests directly from your device, so your key may be visible in browser or network activity. 6. Children ForexAI is a financial-analysis tool and is not designed specifically for children. 7. Changes This Privacy Policy may be updated as ForexAI develops.
